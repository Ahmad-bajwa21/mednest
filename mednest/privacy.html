MedNest
Your family's health, in one place — and only on your phone.
Last updated: 10 October 2026 · Version 1.0

In short

  • Your health data stays on your phone. MedNest has no account, no login, and no server that stores your records.
  • The AI runs on your device. Reading documents, writing summaries and answering your questions all happen offline, on the phone itself.
  • We cannot see your records. There is no way for the app to send your health documents, medicines or results to us or anyone else.
  • You are in control. You can delete everything at any time, and backups are encrypted with a password only you hold.

1. Who we are

MedNest ("the app", "we", "us") is published by Gieek Software Solutions. You can contact us at gieeksoftwaresolutions@gmail.com.

2. What MedNest is

MedNest is a personal health-record organiser. You photograph your family's medical papers (prescriptions, lab reports, scans, bills) and the app organises them for each person, reminds you about medicines, and prepares a one-page summary you can show a doctor.

MedNest does not diagnose, does not recommend or change treatment, and does not replace a doctor. It records, organises and explains what is already written on your own documents.

3. What data the app holds, and where

All of the following is created and stored only on your device, inside the app's private, encrypted storage. None of it is collected by us or sent to any server:

  • Profiles you create (name, relation, birth year, sex, blood group, allergies).
  • Photographs of documents and the text read from them.
  • Extracted medicines, doses, test results and dates.
  • Reminders, dose logs, pattern notes and summaries.

The on-device database is encrypted, and document images are encrypted in the app's private storage. The encryption keys are held in the device's secure hardware keystore.

4. How the AI works

MedNest uses an on-device AI model (downloaded once during setup) together with on-device text recognition. All processing happens locally. Your documents, questions and results are never sent to a cloud AI service, and we receive nothing about what the AI reads or answers.

5. Permissions we ask for

  • Camera — only to photograph your documents. Photos are saved locally.
  • Notifications — to show your medicine and follow-up reminders.
  • Exact alarms — so reminders fire on time.
  • Photos (system picker) — only the images you pick are imported; we do not scan your gallery.

MedNest does not request location, contacts, SMS or microphone-for-recording permissions for its core function.

6. Internet use

MedNest works fully offline. The only times it uses the internet are optional and never include your health data:

  • Downloading the AI model once during setup.
  • Showing ads (see section 7), if you have not removed them.
  • Processing an in-app purchase through Google Play.
  • Backing up to your own Google Drive, if you turn backup on (see section 8).

7. Advertising

The free version shows banner ads supplied by Google AdMob, on a small number of list screens only. Ads never appear on screens that show your documents, medicines, results, chat or summaries.

  • Ads are requested as non-personalised.
  • No health data and no profile information is ever passed to the ad provider.
  • The Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect device identifiers and technical data to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's own policies. Where required, we show a consent prompt.
  • You can permanently remove ads with a one-time in-app purchase.

8. Backup and sharing

Backup is optional. If you enable it, MedNest writes a single encrypted file to your own Google Drive app-data folder, protected by a password you set. We never see this file and cannot open it. If you forget the password, the backup cannot be recovered.

If you share a profile to another phone, MedNest creates an encrypted .mednest file protected by a code you share separately. Sharing sends a copy; it is not live sync.

9. Deleting your data

You can delete any document or profile at any time, which removes its images, medicines, results, reminders and notes. Settings → Delete all data permanently wipes the entire database, all images and the encryption keys from the device. Because we never hold your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

10. Children

You may create a profile for a child in your family to keep their records. The app is intended to be operated by an adult caretaker. We do not knowingly collect any data from children directly — all data stays on the caretaker's device.

11. Crash reporting

If crash reporting is included in a build, it is opt-in and is configured to strip all user text and health content. It never transmits your documents, medicines or results.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, where appropriate, notify you in the app.
